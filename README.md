### Featured Projects

#### 🛰️ AI-Driven GNSS Positioning for Dynamic Vehicle Navigation

A **research project** completed during my internship at **Resolve South
(SUPARCO)**, focused on improving GNSS positioning accuracy for dynamic
vehicle navigation using Machine Learning.

- Worked with multi-source GNSS data: **SPP, L5, B2B, and NRTK**.
- Consolidated GNSS logs from three vehicle speeds **(10, 20, and 30 km/h)**
  into a timestamp-aligned dataset of **1,669 records**.
- Used **NRTK as the high-accuracy reference** for evaluation.
- Applied **XGBoost regression models** for B2B positioning correction.
- Achieved approximately **94% reduction in horizontal positioning error**
  and **96% reduction in vertical error** on held-out test data.
- Explored **LSTM-based trajectory reconstruction** during simulated
  **60-second GNSS outages**.
- Combined LSTM predictions with an **Adaptive Kalman Filter** to refine
  the reconstructed vehicle trajectory.
- Evaluated the approaches against reference positioning and unseen route data.

**Tech Stack:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, XGBoost, LSTM,
Adaptive Kalman Filter, GNSS, Machine Learning
